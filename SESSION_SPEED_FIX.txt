2026-10-27

WHAT CHANGED
- Editing and Undo snapshots share immutable original image strings, while
  editable objects, points, masks, metadata and references remain detached.
- History compares state without serializing every photo twice per edit.
- Autosave reuses unchanged detached tab sessions and old Undo entries, so
  clearing a project or working in another tab does not keep reprocessing
  the previous drawing history. Mutable storage callers retain exact captures.
- Identical background job polls no longer rewrite storage and refresh every
  subscribed editor view. Real queue transitions and results still update.
- Decoded image, erased-mask, expanded-region and thumbnail caches have limits.
  Starting/resetting a workspace clears derived data. In-flight decode callers
  still receive their own image without repopulating retired caches.
- Private rendering canvases are released immediately after use. Closing
  Before/after releases its hidden full-size comparison canvas.
- Immutable source validation is reused within a bounded cache. Changed bytes,
  dimensions, metadata and invalid input are still checked; reset clears it.

PRESERVED
Original source bytes, references, exact masks, Undo/Redo, editable projects,
tabs, saved history, queued submissions, generation model/quality/prompt and
reference mappings. No saved projects or production jobs were deleted, and no
paid generation or reviewer request was made for these tests.

MEASURED IN ISOLATED BROWSER FIXTURES
60 Undo entries with 4 strokes of 1500 points per entry:
- Save after clearing the project: about 818–1108 ms before, 22–23 ms after
  (totals include a 20 ms measurement wait).
- Worst event-loop pause in that fixture: 1022 ms before, 5.8 ms after.

2048x1536 photo, 12 references, 8 labels, mask and 30 repeated edits:
- Editing long tasks: 31 before, 2 after; total 3792 ms before, 123 ms after.
- Browser JavaScript heap after clearing: about 488 MB before, 27 MB after.
  Heap readings depend on garbage collection and are not total computer RAM.
- Exact original/reference strings and masks recovered through tab switching,
  Undo and Redo. Blank idle and typing had no long tasks in this fixture.
- Final isolated pan/drag/mask check: longest pointer-move handlers were
  2.1/2.5/0.8 ms. Full-resolution rendering still occasionally produced roughly
  100 ms tasks while masking. All six 16-point strokes survived reset/recovery.

These measurements describe the fixtures, not guaranteed frame rates for every
image size, layer count, machine or paid provider generation time.
