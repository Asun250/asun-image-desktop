Asun IMG desktop 1.6.7 - view and image-layer rotation
2026-10-23

ROTATE THE WORKSPACE FOR MASKING
Choose Rotate view beside Fit (rotation icon + angle on compact windows).
Drag the angle slider, enter an angle, turn left/right 90 degrees or Reset 0 degrees.
This rotates the workspace only. It does not change saved or exported image pixels.
Mask, Restore, manual selections, highlights, detected areas and their guides remain aligned.
Fit and zoom account for the view angle. Rulers are hidden until the view returns to 0 degrees.

ROTATE AN IMAGE IN THE OUTPUT
Select an image layer, then choose Properties > Rotate image layer.
The slider, exact angle, 90-degree buttons and Reset rotate around the image's center.
An adjustment previews continuously and commits as one Undo step.
The layer angle is editable, saved in native projects/variants and used in exports and generation.
Original photo bytes and masks remain intact. Bound number labels follow the image.
Independent text/pencil/highlight layers keep their document positions.
The white paper in Draw / sketch reference is fixed; placed photo layers can rotate.

CANVAS SIZE
Rotation does not resize the canvas. Image corners beyond the canvas are clipped.
Fit photo includes the full rotated photo in a resized canvas.
If restoring a stretched, rotated photo's native aspect would require shearing other layers,
Fit photo reports that limitation rather than silently changing their shapes.

LOADING THE UPDATE
Save the project in the existing desktop window, then refresh that window.
The update does not reload the user's window or change the phone/web app.
